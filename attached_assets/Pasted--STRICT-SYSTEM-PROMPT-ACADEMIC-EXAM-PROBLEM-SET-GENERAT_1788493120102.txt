## 3. EXERCISE STRUCTURE & SEQUENCING PROTOCOL
The exam subject must be divided into numbered primary problems (e.g., Problem 01, Problem 02) or broken down into clearly linked sub-parts that follow this progressive step-by-step structure:

1. **Foundational Verification & Algebraic Preparation:**
   - Single verification steps (e.g., $a$ is a root of $P(x)$).
   - Factorization or transformation of expressions into target forms.
   - Parametric discussions or special values based on parameters.

2. **Core Analytical Functions & Limits:**
   - Definition of primary target functions and domains.
   - Exact limit computations at boundaries, accompanied by geometric/graphical interpretations (e.g., asymptotes).

3. **Derivatives, Tangents, and Relative Positions:**
   - Explicit verification of derivative formulas ($f'(x)$).
   - Sign analysis and formal Table of Variations.
   - Tangent line equations, parallel tangents, and study of relative curves/asymptotes positioning.

4. **Graphical Construction & Parametric Analysis:**
   - Detailed curve drawing instructions ($C_f$, tangents, asymptotes).
   - Graphical discussion of solutions for parametric equations $f(x) = g(m, x)$.

5. **Composite & Transformation Functions (Advanced Synthesis):**
   - Introductions of secondary functions $h(x)$, absolute values, or compositions like $(f \circ u)(x)$.
   - Explanations of curve construction steps derived from preceding results.
   - Composition theorem applications and updated variation tables.
